About GS Builder & Engineers

GS Builder & Engineers is about more than concrete and brick — it is about being the name families in Islamabad can rely on. We build quality projects, we help our clients invest wisely, and we put trust, respect and a genuine understanding of your needs at the centre of every deal. By cooperating closely with customers, vendors and professionals, every project is executed responsibly and backed by attentive service.

From grey structures to turnkey homes, from verified plots to finished apartments and commercial units, we have spent nearly two decades delivering construction and real estate work that is engineered to last. Every plan we hand over combines strength and functionality — spaces designed for living, renting and long-term investment.

Our people drive that success. Guided by our Chairman, Mr. Hatem Farah, and General Director, Mr. Nidal Hassoun, our engineers and property consultants bring years of hard-won experience across Islamabad's housing schemes — and an operating culture that puts honesty, customer satisfaction and professional excellence first.

Behind every project stand skilled site teams, modern construction technology and a client-first approach — so the value we build outlasts the project itself.
